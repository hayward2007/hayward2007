About

"I'd rather ship something people actually use than something that only gets graded."

I'm Hayward Kim — Kim Hyeong-seok — and I work at the point where robotics, AI, and software meet the people who end up using them.

I graduated from the Hacking Defense department at Korea Digital Media High School, 22nd cohort, and I'm currently at Korea University's College of Engineering.

Most of what I build starts as a competition entry — a six-legged robot, an AI consumption coach, an unmanned delivery bot — and the ones that hold up keep going. DIMIPAY KIOSK still runs payments at my old high school; AIO is still the app students use to get through the day.

Along the way that track record earned the 2024 Korea Talent Award, 30 competition entries, and a handful of stages — from an EBS broadcast panel to a robotics collaboration with NAVER LABS.
